Privacy Policy

Time to Surface — technical dive planner

Last updated: 20 August 2026

Time to Surface collects nothing. This document exists because Apple requires every app to have one (App Store Review Guideline 5.1.1(i)), not because there is much to disclose.

What is collected

Nothing. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party code of any kind in this app.

Network access

The app makes no network requests. It contains no networking code at all. Nothing you enter can leave your device, because there is no mechanism by which it could.

What is stored, and where

  • Your divers and their consumption figures — the people you plan for, the breathing rates you have entered, and the dates you gave those figures.
  • Your settings — units, gradient factors, oxygen limits, ascent and descent rates, and the gas strategy you last planned on.

Dive plans themselves are not stored. The app works one plan out at a time and forgets it when you change the inputs.

All of it is written to a single file inside the app's own storage on your iPhone. It is included in your device backup, so restoring a new phone brings it back. If you back your phone up to iCloud, Apple's backup includes this file — that is between you and Apple, and it is how your data follows you to a new device. Nothing is sent to us, because there is no "us" to send it to.

Deleting it

Settings › Clean all stored data from my phone clears your divers, their consumption history and your settings, and returns the app to a first launch. Deleting the app removes the file with it. Neither leaves a copy anywhere, because no copy was ever made.

Children

The app is a planning tool for certified technical divers and is not directed at children. It collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version appears here with a new date. Since the app collects nothing, any change is likely to be a clarification rather than a new disclosure.
